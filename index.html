Market breadth · Stockbee MM reference

Timing context · not a buy signal by itself

Stockbee Market Monitor (MM) by Pradeep Bonde tracks breadth of major moves (how many stocks are making large % moves) to help time risk-on / risk-off phases. It is a market-timing reference — not a stock pick list and not invented here.

Official live MM numbers come from Stockbee MM or your own scans — MarketDesk does not fabricate MM readings. Public explainers: What you need to know about market breadth, How to use market breadth, Market Monitor scans.

Classic MM column names (cheat-sheet)

ColumnWhat it counts
4% up / 4% down (daily)# of common stocks up/down ≥4% on high volume that day
10-day cumulative breadth ratio(sum of 4%+ days over 10) ÷ (sum of 4%− days over 10)
25%± quarter# up/down ≥25% over a quarter
25% / 50% month# with large monthly moves
34/13 bull & bear# up/down ≥13% over 34 days
MMA+ / MMA−% of stocks in confirmed uptrend / downtrend

Values above are labels only. Fill them from Stockbee or your scanner — never treat empty slots as zeros.
